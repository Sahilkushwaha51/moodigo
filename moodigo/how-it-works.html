How Moodigo Works

Your mood is the starting point. Moodigo turns the way you feel into travel inspiration and helps you discover places that match your mood, vibe and travel style.

01

Choose Your Mood

Tell Moodigo how you are feeling. Choose a mood that matches what you want from your next journey.

02

Tell Us Your Vibe

Answer a few simple questions about your travel preferences, such as travelling solo or with friends and the kind of experience you want.

03

Get Matched Places

Moodigo uses your answers to show destinations that fit the mood and travel experience you are looking for.

04

Start Exploring

Explore your recommended destinations, discover new possibilities and find the journey that feels right for you.
