Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Moodigo may collect, use and protect information when you use our website.

Last updated: September 2026

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes the general privacy practices of Moodigo ("we", "us" or "our") when you visit or use the Moodigo website. By using the website, you acknowledge this policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use Moodigo, we may collect information such as:

  • Information you provide when creating an account, such as your name and email address.
  • Login information needed to access your account.
  • Your selected mood, travel preferences and answers to recommendation questions.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device information and basic website usage data.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Moodigo features.
  • Create travel recommendations based on your selections.
  • Maintain and improve the website and its functionality.
  • Help protect the website from misuse, security issues or unauthorized activity.
  • Communicate with you when necessary regarding your account or the website.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Moodigo may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, support website functionality and understand how visitors use the website. You can control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

5. How We Protect Information

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no method of storing or transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to operate the website, provide requested services, comply with applicable law, or protect the rights, safety and security of Moodigo and its users.

7. Third-Party Links

Moodigo may contain links to third-party websites or services. Those websites have their own privacy practices and policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

8. Your Choices

Depending on the features available on the website, you may be able to update account information, control certain browser settings or choose whether to provide particular information. Some information may be required for specific features to work.

9. Children's Privacy

Moodigo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to the website, please contact us so the information can be reviewed and handled appropriately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the website, its features or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Moodigo's privacy practices, please contact the Moodigo website administrator through the contact method provided on the website.
